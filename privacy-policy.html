PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

Welcome to WEEBFOUR (accessible from https://weebfour.com), owned and operated by Balaji Kishore (trading and operating as WEEBFOUR). Your privacy and data security are of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy documents the types of information we collect, how it is processed, and the measures we take to safeguard your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit or place an order on our store, we collect the necessary personal details to fulfill your streetwear purchase:

  • Contact Information: Full name, mobile phone number, and email address.
  • Delivery Information: Shipping address, apartment/flat number, city, state, postal code, and delivery landmarks.
  • Transaction Data: Order details, items purchased, total amount, transaction identifiers, and payment method selected.
  • Device & Browsing Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and pages visited on our storefront to optimize site performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for legitimate e-commerce operations, including:

  • Processing, packaging, and dispatching your streetwear orders.
  • Sending automated order confirmations, tax invoices, and courier tracking details via email (weebfour.4@gmail.com) and SMS / WhatsApp.
  • Providing responsive customer support for any sizing, shipment, or delivery questions.
  • Preventing fraudulent transactions and ensuring overall website security.

3. Payment Security & Processing (Cashfree Gateway)

All online payments (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, NetBanking, Wallets) on WEEBFOUR are processed securely through RBI-licensed payment aggregators, including Cashfree Payments India Private Limited.

  • Zero Card Storage: WEEBFOUR does not store, process, or have access to your full credit card numbers, debit card PINs, CVVs, or bank net banking credentials.
  • PCI-DSS Level 1 Encryption: All transaction communications are secured via bank-grade 256-bit Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) encryption adhering to global PCI-DSS standards.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share necessary data with trusted logistics and technical service partners under strict confidentiality:

  • Logistics Partners: Delhivery, Blue Dart, DTDC, Xpressbees (for physical doorstep package delivery).
  • Payment Processors: Cashfree (for secure, encrypted payment clearance).
  • Legal Authorities: Only when strictly required by applicable Indian laws, judicial orders, or governmental authorities.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use essential functional cookies and local storage tokens to store your shopping bag items, remember customer checkout preferences, and deliver smooth UI transitions. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though some storefront features (such as keeping items in your bag) may be affected.

6. Your Data Rights

You have the right to inspect, update, or request the deletion of your personal contact data stored on our systems at any time. Simply contact our support team at weebfour.4@gmail.com.
