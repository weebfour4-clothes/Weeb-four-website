SHIPPING & DELIVERY POLICY

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

At WEEBFOUR (https://weebfour.com), we provide fast, reliable, and secure doorstep express courier shipping across India.

1. Pan-India Delivery Coverage

We deliver to 19,000+ PIN codes across all states and Union Territories in India through Tier-1 express courier partners: Delhivery, Blue Dart, DTDC, and Xpressbees.

Metro Cities
Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune:
2 – 4 Business Days
Rest of India
Tier-2, Tier-3 cities, regional towns, and rural areas:
4 – 7 Business Days

2. Order Processing & Dispatch Timeline

  • All orders placed before 3:00 PM IST are processed and packed on the same business day.
  • Standard order dispatch timeframe is within 24 to 48 hours (excluding national holidays and Sundays).
  • Once your streetwear is packed and picked up by our courier partner, you will receive an automated tracking link via SMS, WhatsApp, and email (weebfour.4@gmail.com).

3. Shipping Charges

We offer FREE EXPRESS COURIER SHIPPING on all prepaid orders across India. Any applicable Cash on Delivery (COD) handling charges will be clearly displayed at checkout before order confirmation.

4. Live Tracking Your Shipment

You can track the live transit status of your package at any time using the direct tracking link provided in your order confirmation email or SMS. You can also message our WhatsApp support at +91 63811 77617 with your Order ID for real-time tracking assistance.

5. Non-Delivery / Address Incomplete

Our courier partners will attempt delivery up to three (3) times. Please ensure your contact phone number is reachable so the delivery partner can coordinate smoothly. If a package returns due to an incorrect or unreachable address, our support team will contact you to reschedule dispatch.
