CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

Thank you for shopping at WEEBFOUR (https://weebfour.com), owned and operated by Balaji Kishore (trading and operating as WEEBFOUR). As an independent limited-edition streetwear brand, every drop is crafted with heavyweight premium fabrics and custom cuts. Please read our cancellation, return, and refund terms below.

1. Order Cancellation Policy

Customers can request order cancellation before the package has been dispatched from our fulfillment facility:

  • Cancellation Window: You may cancel your order within 2 to 4 hours of placement, or anytime prior to handover to our courier partner.
  • How to Cancel: Contact us immediately with your Order ID (#WEEB-XXXX) via WhatsApp at +91 63811 77617 or email weebfour.4@gmail.com.
  • Cancellation Refund: For prepaid orders cancelled prior to dispatch, a 100% full refund is initiated immediately and credited to the original payment source within 5-7 business days via Cashfree.

2. Returns & Replacement Policy

Limited Edition Streetwear Notice: Due to the exclusive, small-batch nature of our drops, we do not accept arbitrary returns or exchanges once an item has been dispatched and delivered in perfect condition. Please review our accurate size chart before ordering.

However, we stand 100% behind the quality and authenticity of our streetwear. We provide immediate replacements or refunds in the following scenarios:

  • Damaged in Transit: If your apparel arrives visibly torn, stained, or damaged.
  • Manufacturing Defect: If there is a stitch unraveling, graphic misprint, or structural fabric flaw.
  • Incorrect Item or Size Sent: If you received a different design or size than what you ordered.

3. How to Claim a Replacement or Defect Refund

To report an issue with your received package:

  • Notify our team within 48 hours of package delivery.
  • Provide clear photos or a short unboxing video highlighting the issue, along with your Order ID and contact details.
  • Send details to our official email weebfour.4@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp at +91 63811 77617.
  • Once verified, our team will dispatch a brand-new replacement at zero extra cost, or process a full refund to your original payment method.

4. Refund Processing Timeline

Once a refund is approved by WEEBFOUR:

  • The refund is processed via our secure payment gateway partner (Cashfree Payments).
  • Prepaid Orders (UPI, Cards, NetBanking): Funds will reflect in your bank account, credit card, or UPI wallet within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank's processing cycle.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: If applicable, refunds are transferred directly to your verified UPI ID or Bank NEFT upon receipt of your account details.
